## What is this study?

We are inviting your child to take part in a research study of AURA, a
tablet app that teaches early number skills. AURA presents the same lesson
in a few different ways that are already standard, everyday teaching
approaches (for example, showing the answer and letting the help fade away,
versus letting your child try first and correcting gently) — and figures
out, for your child specifically, which approach seems to help them learn
fastest and remember longest. It does this by trying different approaches
in a fair, alternating way at first, and then leaning more and more toward
whichever one is actually working for your child.

This is **not** a medical or diagnostic study. AURA does not diagnose,
treat, or measure autism, emotions, or mental health in any way, and it
never asks your child to stop or change any behaviour that isn't part of
the lesson itself (including things like stimming). It is an educational
technology study.

## What would my child do?

If you agree, your child would use the AURA tablet app during their normal
instructional time at `[PLACEHOLDER — site name]`, for about `[PLACEHOLDER

An adult who knows your child will check, every single session, that your
child seems willing to take part — not just once at the start. If your
child seems reluctant, upset, or unwilling at any point, the session stops
immediately, no questions asked, and this has no effect on their regular
instruction.

## What information is collected?

- Which teaching approach was used and how your child responded (correct,
  how long they took, whether they finished) — this is the core of what
  makes the comparison meaningful.
- Your child's nickname and age (year and month only — not their exact
  birthdate).
- We do **not** collect your child's home address or school records, and the
  app **never uses a camera or microphone**: no video, photos or audio of
  your child are recorded, ever. (Photos you choose to add for your child's
  Talk board or stories stay on your own device and are never uploaded.)

## What are the risks?

This is considered **minimal risk** research — about the same as your child
using any educational app. The main things we thought carefully about:

- **A teaching approach that doesn't suit your child, before the app has
  figured that out.** The app automatically watches for signs that your
  child might be getting frustrated (based on things like how they're
  answering, not appearance or behaviour) and switches to a calmer, safer
  approach on its own if it sees that pattern — this is a real, always-on
  safety feature of the software, not just a promise.
- **Screen time / sensory comfort.** Sessions are short and the app learns
  and adapts to how long your child actually stays engaged.
- **Privacy.** See "What information is collected?" above and the separate
  privacy notice we've given you — you're in control of what's shared and
  can request full deletion at any time.

## What are the benefits?

Your child may benefit from a lesson experience that adapts to them faster
than a one-size-fits-all lesson would — but we can't promise this, and your
child's regular instruction continues exactly as it would without the
study either way. More broadly, this research may help teachers and
researchers better understand how to individualize teaching approaches for
autistic children in the future.

## Taking part is completely voluntary

You can say no, or change your mind and withdraw your child, at any time,
for any reason, with **no effect whatsoever** on your child's education,
services, or relationship with `[PLACEHOLDER — site name]`. If you
withdraw, you can choose to have all of your child's data permanently
deleted (this is the default option we'll offer you), or, if you separately
agree, to let already-collected anonymized data stay in the research
dataset.
